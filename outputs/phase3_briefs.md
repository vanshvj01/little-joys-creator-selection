# Creator briefs — Little Joys

Five creators, in score order. Every number below is computed by `src/phase3_briefs.py` from the raw CSVs; claims are limited to ones Little Joys has already run in its ads.

## Viha Dikonda — @storiesbyvihaa

**Product:** Proteinmix (in Viha's roti / paratha)  
**Score:** 0.72 (fit 0.58 · reach 0.79 · safety 0.93) — model rank #1, in the top 5 in 97% of weight perturbations (robust)

### Why them

- Viha is the presenter in 70% of posts — the child-led format behind LJ-004 (the account's best ad, ₹140 per purchase) and LJ-050.
- Her single most-played recent post is Viha trying to speak Hindi in a Telugu accent (6.5M plays), so a Hinglish script plays to an existing strength.
- She already films Viha eating and reacting to food (e.g. the restaurant spicy-food reaction, 3.3M plays).
- She has worked with Little Joys before: the audience file lists it among her past-year brands. No ad in this export names her, so whichever ad that was cannot be identified here; ask the brand team how it performed before booking.
- Followers 827,496; typical video reach 771k-3,096k plays (middle half of their last 16 videos).

### Risk to check first

- Her audience is only 30% women (70% men; largest age group 25-34, at 44%). The buyer is usually a mother, so a large share of her reach may never buy. This is the only one of the five with audience data, so it is shown as a risk, not scored. Ask for her current audience split before booking.

### Suggested hook

Viha, to camera, holding her plate, in her Telugu-accented Hinglish: "Aaj dekho meri plate mein kya kya hai!" On screen: "Viha ki roti mein ek secret hai 🤫". Spoken and on-screen together, as LJ-004 opened.

### Script outline

| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0-3s | Viha with her lunch plate, spoken + text hook (above). |
| 3-10s | Cut to Mom's hands adding one scoop of Proteinmix to the atta; Viha peeks in suspiciously. |
| 10-20s | Viha bites the roti, does her signature exaggerated chewing face, then: "Taste toh bilkul same hai!" This is the core moment — a real child genuinely tasting on camera. |
| 20-30s | Mom voiceover (Hinglish): 12g plant protein in one scoop, from pea, brown rice and soy; no change in taste. |
| 30-35s | Viha asks for a second roti; packshot with "Little Joys Proteinmix" on screen. |

### Claims to make (all already used in Little Joys ads)

- Proteinmix: no change in taste or smell of roti / pancakes — used in 8 ads (together ₹170 per purchase), e.g. LJ-004, LJ-097, LJ-056. Ads using it were cheaper than the Proteinmix average (₹190).
- Proteinmix: made from pea, brown rice and soy protein — used in 8 ads (together ₹160 per purchase), e.g. LJ-004, LJ-098, LJ-052. Ads using it were cheaper than the Proteinmix average (₹190).
- Proteinmix: 12g plant protein in one scoop — used in 16 ads (together ₹180 per purchase), e.g. LJ-004, LJ-097, LJ-056. No better than the Proteinmix average (₹190): fine to say, don't lead with it.

### What to avoid

- An adult script read by Viha about nutrients (her profile: an adult reading marketing copy feels foreign).
- Studio lighting; keep the home-video look.
- Height / 'overall growth' promises and deficiency fear (both did worse in the ads).
- Pure Hindi: the 10 pure-Hindi ads cost ₹780 per purchase vs ₹300 for Hinglish (not conclusive, but no reason to risk it). Keep English words in the script.
- Telugu as the only version — no Telugu ad has ever been tested; regional-language ads cost 47% more per purchase after adjustment.

### Format

30-35s Reel, 9:16, home setting, Hinglish (Hindi-English mix, not pure Hindi). A Telugu cut of the same video can run as a small side test.

### Expected performance (and how sure we are)

- No ad by this creator is in the data, so there is no direct measurement. The estimate comes from the 7 Proteinmix ads built the same way (a child reacts to the taste on camera, spoken in English or Hinglish).
- Those ads cost ₹170 per purchase together (90% range ₹140-₹420), against ₹190 for all 18 Proteinmix ads and ₹410 for the whole account.
- Without the single best ad (LJ-004) the lookalikes cost ₹300 (₹270-₹560) against ₹350 for Proteinmix overall.
- Individual lookalike ads ranged from ₹140 to ₹1,050: execution matters as much as the recipe. Most of the advantage over the account average comes from Proteinmix itself being cheap to sell; the format adds about 10% on top. Read this as a direction, not a forecast.
- Organic reach: their typical video gets 771k-3,096k plays; too few sponsored videos to say whether sponsored posts get fewer plays.

- Ads: LJ-004 (₹140/purchase), LJ-056 (₹270/purchase), LJ-098 (₹350/purchase), LJ-050 (₹250/purchase)
- Creator posts: https://www.instagram.com/p/DdfhprMBiO0/, https://www.instagram.com/p/DdqBtBgqW-q/, https://www.instagram.com/p/DeIwMiYhtVN/

- Individual lookalike ads ranged from ₹250 to ₹730: execution matters as much as the recipe. Ads in this format were 25% cheaper per purchase than the Nutrimix average. Read this as a direction, not a forecast.
